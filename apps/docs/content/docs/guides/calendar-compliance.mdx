## Colours on the cards

A planned session gets a coloured strip on its left once it can be judged:

| Strip | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Green | Done within 20% of the planned duration |
| Yellow | Done at 50 to 79%, or 121 to 150%, of the plan |
| Orange | Done more than 50% away from the plan |
| Red | Missed: its day is over and no activity is linked |

The duration is compared first; sessions planned only in distance are
compared on distance. A session done without a duration or distance goal is
green. Sessions still to do, and activities done without a plan, have no
strip. The tooltip of a card gives the status and the percentage.

The strip needs the activity to be linked to its session. Synced activities
are linked automatically when they match a session of the same day; otherwise
drag the activity onto its session, or see
[Link a completed activity to a session](/docs/guides/link-activity-suggestions).

## Weekly summary

Next to each week (or below it, in the week view), the summary shows:

- duration, distance and elevation, **done / planned**, with a progress bar;
- the training load done / planned against the recommended range: the
  lighter part of the bar is the load still planned. Bars under it show the
  load day by day, lighter for the sessions still to do;
- the sessions done, missed and still to do;
- **TSB**, the form at the end of the week (fitness minus fatigue), marked
  *projected* until the week is over.

The **Display** menu (gear icon) chooses which of these rows show. The chart
of weekly load and form over several months is in the
[season view](/docs/guides/calendar-season-and-display).

## How planned load is estimated

Planned sessions and races are estimated in the same unit as the load of your
activities (Banister TRIMP), using the first method that applies:

1. **Workout heart-rate targets**: if every timed step of a structured workout
   has a heart-rate target (explicit bpm, heart-rate zone, % HR max, or % HR
   reserve), load is summed step by step over the workout.
2. **AI fallback**: if a structured workout uses targets that cannot be
   resolved from heart rate alone (such as pace, power, or distance steps) and
   AI load estimation is enabled, the AI estimates the session's load.
3. **Duration and target RPE**: for unstructured sessions, races, or when AI is
   unavailable, load is estimated from the planned duration and target RPE.

Deterministic estimation (heart-rate targets or duration and RPE) requires
both your maximum and resting heart rates (`HR_MAX` and `HR_REST`) in
**Metrics**, plus either heart-rate targets on every workout step (with your
heart-rate zones configured when steps target zones) or a planned duration and
target RPE.
